This Privacy Policy describes how Chronote ("we", "our", or "the Plugin"), an Obsidian plugin, handles your data, emails, and tracking. We take user privacy seriously and operate under a strict "privacy-first" model.
1. Data Collection and Usage
Local Data (Obsidian Vault)
• Your Notes: Chronote operates locally within your Obsidian application. The plugin reads your notes only to perform the tasks you explicitly request (e.g., generating flashcards or scheduling study sessions).
• Local Storage: All plugin configurations, settings, and temporary session data are stored locally on your device within your Obsidian global settings.
Personal Data & Subscriptions
Note: The payment system to access our Cloud AI features is currently under development and not yet active. Once implemented:
• We will collect your Email Address, Name, and Billing Address via a secure third-party payment processor.
• We will never see or store your credit card or debit card numbers. All payments will be securely processed by a professional third-party merchant.
2. Emails and Communication
• Transactional Emails: Once our payment system is active, you agree to receive essential transactional emails (delivery of your unique License Key, renewal reminders, and invoices).
• No Spam / Newsletters: We will never use your email address to send you unsolicited marketing materials or third-party offers unless you explicitly opt-in to a newsletter.
• No Selling of Data: Your email address and personal details will never be sold, rented, or shared with any third-party advertisers.
3. Tracking, Analytics, and Cookies
• No User Tracking: We do not use any digital analytics software (such as Google Analytics or PostHog) within the plugin to track your behavior, keystrokes, or usage patterns.
• No Advertising Pixels: We do not have the Facebook Pixel or any other marketing/retargeting scripts installed.
• No Web Cookies / Beacons: Since Chronote runs as a local Obsidian plugin, it does not use browser cookies or web beacons to track your digital footprint.
4. Third-Party Integration & APIs
Cloud AI Services
To provide AI-powered features (such as automated testing and study planning), the plugin sends selected text segments to our secure Cloud API (utilizing open-source language models).
• Data sent to the Cloud AI API is processed transiently and is never used to train future AI models.
• Your requests are authenticated using your unique License Key to monitor fair-use caps.
Google Calendar API
Chronote allows you to sync your study schedule with your Google Calendar.
• Authentication: The plugin uses Google OAuth to connect directly from your device to Google Calendar. We do not store your Google password.
• Data Access: The plugin only accesses your Google Calendar to create, update, or delete study events generated from your notes.
• Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or share any data obtained through the Google Calendar API with third parties.
5. Compliance (GDPR & CCPA)
• GDPR (European Union): If you are a resident of the EU, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase the personal data we hold about you (primarily your email and subscription info).
• CCPA (California): We do not sell your personal information. You have the right to request what data we collect and ask for its deletion.
6. Children's Privacy
Our service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
